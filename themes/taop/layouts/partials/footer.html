FAQ
What if the book isn’t what I expected it would be?
The Art of PostgreSQL is a book that teaches SQL to application developers. If it's not of any value to you, then just say so and I'll refund you, no questions asked.
I bought the book, and I'm confused. What now?
The content of the book is available as a PDF and ePub file, and you can also download the SQL queries. The content is available over at https://members.theartofpostgresql.com/courses/the-art-of-postgresql/ from your student dashboard.
I bought a previous edition. Do I get the 2026 update?
Yes — the update is free for all existing purchasers. Log into your ThriveCart account, open the lesson, and download the updated PDF and ePub directly.
