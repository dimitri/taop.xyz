Why fair pricing?

$449 a year is a reasonable price in a high-income country and an unreasonable one elsewhere. Rather than lock people out by geography, the courses are offered at four prices for the same access. Pay based on your local purchasing power.

If you can afford the standard price, please choose it. That is what keeps the lower prices available to everyone else.

Note what this is not: it isn't a cheaper, cut-down tier. Every option here is the Architect tier — the same 48 modules, the same labs, the same book that someone paying $449 receives.
