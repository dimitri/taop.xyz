Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I pick my date?

    You don't need to at checkout. Sign in to your dashboard afterwards and register for whichever available date works for you. If you bought the curriculum, you register once per topic, each on its own schedule.

  • What if I can't make the live session?

    The recording is yours to keep, so you can catch up anytime — no need to rearrange your calendar around the live date.

  • What's the difference between a single session and the curriculum?

    A single session covers one of the six topics below. The curriculum is all six — priced at five, so the sixth session is effectively free.

  • Do I need to be an advanced SQL user?

    These sessions assume you already write SQL in production — SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY — and want to go further. They're not an introduction to SQL.

  • Is the book included?

    Yes — the digital edition of The Art of PostgreSQL (Second Edition, Updated 2026) is included with every Masterclass purchase.
