What a tier actually buys

Async and queue-driven — no calls, no consulting engagement

  • A place in the queue

    Issues and PRs stay on the public GitHub repo. Your tier decides where they sort, across all four projects.

  • Triage on the clock

    Your tier defines the response and triage window — so you know when work starts, not just that it will.

  • A fix upstream, not a fork

    Fixes ship in the next public release, for everyone. You stop maintaining a local patch forever.
