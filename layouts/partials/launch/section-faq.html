FAQ

  • Which PostgreSQL version does the book cover?

    The Second Edition, Updated (2026) covers PostgreSQL 11 through 18. The core skills — window functions, CTEs, aggregates, indexing, data modeling, concurrency — apply directly to every modern PostgreSQL release. The 2026 update adds coverage of features through PG 18, a new chapter on vector search with pgvector, and 56 new figures.

  • What skill level do I need to start?

    You should be comfortable with basic SQL — SELECT, JOIN, WHERE. The book takes you from there. The course and workshops assume you've read the book or have equivalent experience.

  • What's the difference between the book and the course?

    The book is the complete reference — 570 pages, 15 real-world datasets. The course is structured learning across three tiers with video, exercises, and progressive challenges. Most developers start with the book and continue with the course. The lab is free for both.
