  • Is the book still relevant if it was written for PostgreSQL 11?

    Yes. The core skills — window functions, CTEs, aggregates, indexing, data modeling, concurrency — apply directly to every modern PostgreSQL release. SQL doesn't expire. A new edition covering the latest PostgreSQL features is in preparation and will be available free of charge to everyone who purchases today.

  • What skill level do I need to start?

    You should be comfortable with basic SQL — SELECT, JOIN, WHERE. The book takes you from there. The course and workshops assume you've read the book or have equivalent experience.

  • What's the difference between the book and the course?

    The book is the complete reference — 468 pages, 334 queries, 15 datasets. The course is structured learning across three tiers with video, exercises, and progressive challenges. Most developers start with the book and continue with the course. The lab is free for both.
