Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can we mix tiers — some Core seats, some Architect seats?

    Yes. Tell us the split when you check out, or email us directly and we'll set it up.

  • What happens to a seat when someone leaves the company?

    Reassign it to someone else from your dashboard at any time — seats aren't tied to a specific person, just a headcount.

  • Do we need fewer than 10 seats?

    Ten is our smallest team pack. Below that, individual accounts at the regular per-person price usually work out cheaper — happy to help you figure out which makes sense.

  • Do you support invoicing or purchase orders?

    Yes, for Unlimited licenses and larger packs — email us and we'll sort out the paperwork.
