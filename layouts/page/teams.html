Frequently Asked Questions

  • How many seats are included?

    The base annual package includes 5 Architect course seats. Add more teammates anytime from your dashboard, at the same per-seat rate.

  • Can we add the Masterclass Curriculum too?

    Yes — it's an optional add-on with its own seat count, assignable from your dashboard alongside your course seats.

  • What happens to a seat when someone leaves the company?

    If they never logged in, you can fix the assignment and hand that seat to someone else. Once a teammate has actually accessed their content, their access becomes personal — they can even register their own email to keep it — so that specific seat isn't reassignable, but you can always add more.

  • How does renewal work?

    The package runs for one year. A renewal option appears in your team dashboard starting a month before it expires — no surprises, no auto-charges.

  • Can we get time with the author?

    Yes — Office Hours is an optional add-on: a 3-hour video call with Dimitri Fontaine for your team to ask about PostgreSQL, your own queries, or architecture decisions.

  • We already have an Enterprise Edition book license from the First Edition — does this still work?

    Yes. Legacy Enterprise Edition licenses (book only, up to 15 members) work with the same team dashboard — manage your members there, and add course or Masterclass seats as an upgrade whenever you're ready. Sign in or get in touch if you need a hand migrating.

  • What's your refund policy?

    Same as our other products: a full, no-questions-asked refund, even after you've started assigning seats.
