SQL is a language for composing relations. These lessons cover the JOIN
operators that matter most in practice — including the LATERAL join that
unlocks per-row subqueries, and WITH RECURSIVE for graphs and hierarchies —
and the traps (amplification, NULL propagation) that catch developers off guard.
